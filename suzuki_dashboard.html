KEY INSIGHTS

What the Data Tells Us

01
Jimny Premium Demand Surging
Suzuki Jimny exceeded sales target by 420% despite being the most expensive model at PKR 4.5M. This signals strong premium segment demand that Pak Suzuki should capitalize on.
02
Alto Needs Marketing Intervention
Suzuki Alto — traditionally Pak Suzuki's entry-level bestseller — is performing below target at 79.4%. Recommend targeted marketing campaigns in Islamabad and Multan where numbers are weakest.
03
Faisalabad is the Revenue Leader
Despite not being the largest city, Faisalabad generated PKR 11.6B — the highest of all 6 regions. Industrial city purchasing power is underestimated in national sales strategy.
04
Stable Monthly Performance
Monthly sales remain consistent around 2,000 units per month with no major seasonal dips. This indicates strong brand loyalty and steady demand throughout the year.
